## 05 - Engagement Models

### Start your project, your way.

1. **Discovery Workshop** - Cut through the brief in a few focused days. Leave with a direction you can staff and fund.
2. **Project Outsourcing** - A full team, a clear scope. Architecture through deployment with a single accountable lead.
3. **Team Extension** - Add rare or slow-to-hire skills that fold into your delivery rituals.
4. **Dedicated Team** - Engineers who embed as a long-term extension of your organisation.
