The problem

Software projects fail for predictable reasons.

  • Teams too big to stay aligned, too junior to move fast
  • Scope that drifts because nobody owns the architecture
  • Handoffs that fracture context between strategy, design, and code
  • Vendors that invoice first, ask questions never
Our approach

One small team. Strategy, interface, and engineering in the same room.

We stay close to the work from first sketch to production release. Visible progress, no blind spots, and a team that clears roadblocks instead of reporting them.

  • Senior only — no bench, no hand-off to a team you never met
  • Kickoff in two weeks, or we tell you why we're not the right fit
  • You own the code, repos, and decisions from day one
  • Fixed scope, visible progress, nothing invoiced you didn't approve
